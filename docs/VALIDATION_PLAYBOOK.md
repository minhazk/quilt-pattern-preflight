# Validation playbook

## Qualified prospect

An independent English-language quilt-pattern designer who has a completed
manuscript, sells or plans to sell patterns, and already checks arithmetic or
pays for technical editing.

## First offer

> I’m testing a narrow preflight tool that checks the arithmetic consistency of
> completed quilt-pattern documents. It does not replace technical editing. I’m
> offering an operator-reviewed beta check for £20, including one revised
> upload. Would you be willing to test it on a real pattern?

Do not automate unsolicited outreach or scrape social networks. Record source,
consent and objections manually using `outreach-tracker-template.csv`.

## Demonstration

1. State the limitation before showing the sample.
2. Show a source excerpt, confirmed input, exact formula and difference.
3. Explain operator quality control and private retention.
4. Ask for a real £20 test payment; do not substitute praise for purchase.
5. Schedule the 14-day revision and structured feedback.

## Funnel

Qualified prospect → landing visit → checkout start → payment → successful
upload → completed report → revision or repeat purchase.

## Continuation threshold

Continue only with evidence of willingness to pay and usefulness. The target is
at least three customers buying another check, using remaining credits, or
submitting a revision, with at least 70% of paid uploads inside supported scope.
Track finding usefulness, incorrect findings, minutes saved, £20 repurchase
intent, technical-editor usage and requested unsupported constructions.

Stop or narrow the product if extraction or operator correction dominates,
customers will not pay, supported-document rate is low, or qualified domain
expertise proves essential to reliable checks.
