# Product scope

## Supported in v1

- English-language DOCX with normal paragraphs/tables
- PDF with selectable text and a usable text layer
- Imperial inch measurements represented as integers, decimals, common
  fractions or mixed numbers
- Confirmed squares, rectangles, straight WOF strips, repeated identical blocks
- Simple rectangular grids
- Explicit straight sashing and borders when the customer confirms inputs
- Piece-count reconciliation, finished/unfinished relationships, strip yield,
  fabric requirement, grid dimensions, consistency and completeness

Every calculation is limited to customer-confirmed inputs. Uncertain
relationships become a review/unsupported result.

## Explicitly unsupported

- Scanned or image-only PDFs and OCR
- Curves, appliqué, paper piecing, templates and on-point layouts
- Directional-print nesting, fussy cutting, nap, shrinkage or cutting
  optimisation
- Metric patterns
- Inference of ambiguous fabric-to-piece or piece-to-block relationships
- Proving construction feasibility, prose quality, accessibility, legal
  compliance or overall technical correctness

Unsupported content is not silently accepted or converted into an invented
rule. It is identified for professional manual review.

## Positioning

The product is a completed-manuscript arithmetic preflight. It is neither a
pattern generator nor an autonomous technical editor.
