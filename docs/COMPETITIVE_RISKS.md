# Competitive risks

Mathless Quilts is the strongest adjacent incumbent identified for this
validation. Its apparent design-input-to-generated-calculations workflow differs
from this pilot’s completed-manuscript-to-extracted-and-reconciled workflow.
That distinction is a hypothesis, not proven defensibility.

Other quilt design/calculation tools can substitute for parts of the workflow.
Human technical editors are the principal paid substitute and remain necessary.
External-document checking may already be, or may quickly become, a normal
incumbent feature.

The founder does not claim quilting credentials. If reliable checks require
domain judgement that cannot be safely represented as confirmed assumptions and
reviewed deterministic rules, stop rather than manufacture authority.

Do not publish unsupported superiority claims, reproduce proprietary competitor
rules, scrape competitor/customer data, or reverse-engineer private products.
